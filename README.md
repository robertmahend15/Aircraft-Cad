
<h1>Regional Aircraft Concept Design</h1>


<h2>Exterior Aircraft Design</h2>
Designed the exterior architecture of a conceptual regional passenger aircraft, including the fuselage, wing configuration, empennage (tail assembly), and overall aerodynamic layout. Produced high-quality CAD models and photorealistic renderings to visualize the aircraft's external form and configuration.

<h2>Interior Cabin & Cockpit Design</h2>
Developed the internal layout of a regional passenger aircraft, including passenger cabin seating arrangements, aisle configuration, storage considerations, and cockpit architecture. The project focused on balancing passenger comfort, space utilization, accessibility, and flight crew ergonomics within the aircraft envelope.

**Skills: Interior Design, Cabin Layout Development, Surface Modeling, Aircraft Configuration Design, Human Factors Engineering, Aerospace Systems Integration, Fusion 360**
